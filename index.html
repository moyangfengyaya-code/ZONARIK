Important Usage Tip

Ensure the charging port waterproof flap is closed and the filter is properly fitted in the primary filter head prior to use.

Troubleshooting

1. Ensure the unit is fully charged.
2. Check that the filter and nozzle are not clogged with debris.
3. Confirm the motor head is fully submerged in water.
4. Clean or replace the filter sponge if it is dirty.
